How to read a listing

  • The blue panel shows output in amps. Each segment of the gauge is 10A, up to 150A.
  • Pins is the number of terminals on the alternator's plug. Match it to your wiring connector.
  • Brand is the alternator's maker or origin as supplied, for example Valeo, Denso or Mando.
  • Always compare the mounting points and pulley with your old unit before you buy.
